Parallel review surfaces

Assessment, rendering, and handoff

These outputs make the result usable by humans and other agents without weakening the evidence boundary.

Tests

Assess, then propose

  • greenfield/test_index.py indexes downstream tests.
  • greenfield/test_assessment.py produces a revision-aware assessment.
  • Codex proposal stays partial when no qualified downstream evidence exists.
  • Outputs: artifacts/greenfield/pr-49695/test-assessment.json, artifacts/greenfield/pr-49695/test-proposal.json.
Review

Render the decision surface

  • greenfield/pr_review.py renders and validates the review.
  • scripts/render_greenfield_pr_review.py is the operational entry point.
  • Canonical format: docs/review/pr-review-template.md.
  • Outputs: artifacts/greenfield/pr-49695/pr-review.json, artifacts/greenfield/pr-49695/pr-review.md.
Handoff

Hash every completed stage

  • greenfield/flow_handoff.py records inputs, outputs, and SHA-256 hashes.
  • Completed bundles reject reruns or source identity changes.
  • Output: artifacts/greenfield/pr-49695/flow.handoff.json.
